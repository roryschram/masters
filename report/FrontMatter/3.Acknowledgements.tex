\chapter*{Acknowledgements}
At the start of my engineering degree, a friend of my parents, who happens to be an engineer himself, heard that I had been accepted into the electrical engineering faculty at UCT. I asked him for any advice that he might have for me, anything that could potentially help me through the four years that lay ahead of me. He said one thing to me, "Make a group of close engineering friends, stick together, and you'll get each other through". This was some of the best advice that I have ever received. To everyone in the engineering friend group, getting to know everyone over the last few years has been one of the coolest experiences in my life. Thank you for all the memories made. I cannot wait to see what interesting things everyone gets up to in the coming years.

A special mention must be made of all the members of the radar lab on the 7th floor of the Menzies building. Thank you for welcoming me, as an undergrad student, so openly into the lab. Working with everyone in the lab over the last few weeks has been a fantastic experience, and I am truly grateful for all the enthusiastic help that has been offered to me by various members of the lab over the last few weeks.

Lastly, my family. Mum, Dad, Nic, and Mike, without your support, none of this would be possible. Mum and Dad, as I have grown older and hopefully slightly wiser, my appreciation towards your decisions to prioritise the education of us three brothers has grown exponentially. Thank you for all the support over the years, I really appreciate it.
